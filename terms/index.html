Plans and Limits

AskAI offers a Free plan and three subscriptions: AskAI Pro Monthly, AskAI Pro Annual, and AskAI Unlimited. The Free and Pro plans include daily allowances for AI requests, PDF analyses, and PDF questions, shown in the app. The PDF question allowance is a daily total across all your PDFs. Daily allowances reset at midnight UTC.

AskAI Unlimited has no daily allowance for AI requests, PDF analyses, or PDF questions. To keep AskAI reliable for everyone, every plan, including AskAI Unlimited, is subject to technical safeguards against automated, excessive, or abusive use, such as a limit on how quickly requests can be sent.

Food search is limited per hour to prevent abuse. We may adjust plans and limits over time.

Subscriptions and Billing

AskAI Pro Monthly is a monthly auto-renewing subscription. AskAI Pro Annual and AskAI Unlimited are yearly auto-renewing subscriptions. All are purchased through the App Store and billed by Apple. The price and billing period of each are shown in the app before you buy. Your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it in your Apple account settings before the renewal date. You can open those settings from Profile > Manage Subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.

You can move to a higher plan in the app at any time. The change takes effect right away, and Apple adjusts what you pay for the time left on your current plan under its policies. To move to a lower plan, use Manage Subscription; the lower plan starts at your next renewal date.

Deleting your AskAI account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your Apple account settings.
